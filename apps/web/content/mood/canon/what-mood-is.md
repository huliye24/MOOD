# Vision

## An open coordination protocol and digital world for human and machine agency.

MOOD connects humans, AI agents, organizations, capital and network resources through shared rules and public memory.

- Identity
- Contribution
- Proof
- Reputation
- Agents
- Nodes
- Organizations
- Capital Coordination
- Governance
- Settlement

```text
WORLD      meaning · culture · place
PROTOCOL   rules · proof · governance
PORTAL     identity · action · history
```

MOOD is the network layer. Applications are built on top.
