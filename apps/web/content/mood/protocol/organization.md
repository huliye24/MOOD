# Organization Protocol

## Organization is a node. Not the whole network.

Organizations remain valid actors and owners of resources. MOOD coordinates their relationships with other network participants rather than replacing them.

- Company Node
- DAO Node
- Research Node
- Community Node
- Foundation Node

```text
ORGANIZATION
identity · members · capabilities · resources
                        ↓
                  MOOD NETWORK
projects · contributions · evidence · reputation
```

An organization participates in the network but does not own the entire network.
