# Capital Protocol

## Capital is a participant.

Capital provides coordination resources but does not automatically create reputation, rights, or governance authority.

```text
Capital
   +
Contribution
   +
Reputation
   +
Governance
   =
Network Coordination
```

- Capital Nodes
- Project Treasury
- Allocation Rules
- Contribution Records
- Settlement

MOOD is a coordination protocol, not an investment platform. No income, return, treasury, or settlement activity is promised by this document.
