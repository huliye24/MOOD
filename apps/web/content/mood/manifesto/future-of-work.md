# Future of Work

## From employee to participant.

Industrial organizations created the employee model. MOOD explores a network-native model in which work becomes a verifiable contribution history.

### Industrial era

```text
Factory
  ↓
Company
  ↓
Employee
```

### Network era

```text
Network
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Contribution
  ↓
Reputation
```

- Employee → Participant
- Company → Project Network
- Job History → Contribution History
- Resume → Reputation Graph

A person may contribute to multiple projects, organizations and communities without pretending that every relationship is employment.
