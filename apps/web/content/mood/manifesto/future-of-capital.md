# Future of Capital

## Capital becomes a participant—not the network's controller.

Traditional capital coordination:

```text
Investor
  ↓
Company
  ↓
Employee
  ↓
Product
```

MOOD explores:

```text
Capital Node
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Contribution Network
  ↓
Settlement
```

Capital is one form of contribution. Code, research, design, data, compute, community and verification also create value.

> The goal is transparent coordination, not financial speculation.
