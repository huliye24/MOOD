# Why MOOD

## The world has platforms. But coordination remains fragmented.

Today, important relationships stop at organizational and platform boundaries.

```text
People  → Company
Capital → Institution
Data    → Platform
AI      → Organization
```

MOOD explores a different model:

```text
People · AI · Capital · Organizations
                  ↓
             MOOD Network
```

MOOD is not designed to replace every company. It creates a shared layer where contribution, reputation, rights and settlement can become portable.

> How can the world coordinate value without requiring one organization to own the entire relationship?
