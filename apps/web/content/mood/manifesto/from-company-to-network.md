# From Company to Network

The company was the dominant coordination structure of the industrial era.

Company model:

```text
Capital + Employees + Management = Production
```

Network model:

```text
People + AI Agents + Organizations + Capital + Resources
                                              = Open Coordination
```

MOOD does not eliminate organizations.

It transforms organizations into network participants.

A company becomes a node.

A person becomes a contributor.

A project becomes a coordination unit.
