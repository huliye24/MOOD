# Digital Society Manifesto

The internet connected information.

Blockchain connected value.

AI connects intelligence.

MOOD explores the next layer:

Coordination.

A digital society requires more than communication. It requires:

- Identity
- History
- Contribution
- Reputation
- Rights
- Settlement

The future network is not only a place to consume information. It is a place where participants create, verify and coordinate value.
