# Future of Work

Industrial organizations created the employee model.

The employee model enabled large-scale production, but it also concentrated opportunity inside organizations.

MOOD explores a network-native model:

Employee -> Participant

Company -> Project Network

Job History -> Contribution History

Resume -> Reputation Graph

A person may contribute to multiple projects, organizations and communities.

Work becomes a recorded contribution history.
