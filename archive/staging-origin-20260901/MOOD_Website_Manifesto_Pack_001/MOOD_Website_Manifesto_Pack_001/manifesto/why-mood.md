# Why MOOD

The world has powerful platforms, but coordination remains fragmented.

Today:

People belong to companies.
Capital belongs to institutions.
Data belongs to platforms.
AI belongs to organizations.

MOOD explores a different model:

People, AI agents, organizations and resources can participate in an open coordination network.

MOOD is not designed to replace every company.
It creates a shared layer where contribution, reputation and settlement can become portable.

The question:

How can the world coordinate value without requiring one organization to own the entire relationship?
