# Open Coordination

Bitcoin demonstrated that decentralized networks could maintain monetary history.

Ethereum demonstrated that decentralized networks could maintain programmable state.

MOOD explores another layer:

Coordination state.

A coordination network should allow:

- identity
- contribution
- evidence
- reputation
- rights
- settlement

to exist beyond a single platform.

MOOD is a protocol, not only a product.
