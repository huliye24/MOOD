# Future of Capital

Traditional capital coordination:

Investor -> Company -> Employee -> Product

MOOD explores:

Capital Node -> Project -> Contribution Network -> Settlement

Capital is one form of contribution.

Other contributions include:
- code
- research
- design
- data
- compute
- community

The goal is transparent coordination, not financial speculation.
