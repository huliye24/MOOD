# Organization as a Network Node

**Status:** Phase Zero foundational draft

**Authority:** Downstream of [`MOOD_CANON.md`](../../MOOD_CANON.md)

## Abstract

Traditional organizations are the main coordination units of industrial society.

MOOD introduces another model:

An organization is not the owner of all production relationships. It is a node inside a global coordination network.

MOOD does not eliminate companies or deny their ownership of resources. It gives companies and other organizations a protocol role through which their capabilities, contributions, and relationships can be coordinated with other network participants.

## Organization Types

- Company Node
- DAO Node
- Research Node
- Community Node
- Foundation Node
- Individual Collective Node

## Organization Passport

Each organization may have:

- Identity
- Members
- Capabilities
- Contribution history
- Reputation
- Treasury relationships
- Governance rights

The precise passport schema and verification rules remain to be specified.

## Principle

A company is no longer the only place where people belong.

People can belong to organizations while also participating in projects across the network.
