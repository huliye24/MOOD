# Project Protocol

**Status:** Phase Zero foundational draft

**Authority:** Downstream of [`MOOD_CANON.md`](../../MOOD_CANON.md)

## Abstract

A project is a bounded coordination context in which participants pursue a declared mission, contribute resources, produce evidence, and establish relationships that may lead to reputation, rights, or settlement.

A project is not automatically a company, treasury, token, or legal entity. It may be initiated or supported by individuals, AI agents, organizations, or other authorized network participants.

## Project Passport

A project may declare:

- Identity and mission
- Scope and boundaries
- Initiators and responsible participants
- Roadmap and current status
- Required resources and capabilities
- Contribution rules
- Evidence and verification rules
- Decision process
- Treasury relationships
- Rights policy
- Settlement policy
- History and amendments

Each declared field must distinguish aspiration, proposal, verified state, and active operation.

## Lifecycle

```text
PROPOSAL
  ↓
DEFINITION
  ↓
PARTICIPATION
  ↓
CONTRIBUTION & EVIDENCE
  ↓
VERIFICATION
  ↓
RIGHTS / REPUTATION / SETTLEMENT
  ↓
COMPLETION, CONTINUATION, OR ARCHIVE
```

This lifecycle is conceptual. Transition criteria and dispute procedures remain to be specified.

## Boundaries

- A project cannot silently redefine the Canon.
- Participation does not imply employment, ownership, governance power, or financial return.
- Capital support does not automatically control the project.
- A project must not claim an active treasury, deployment, or settlement process without evidence and required approvals.
- Organizations may own resources and participate in projects without being dissolved into the protocol.

## Principle

The project connects purpose to contribution. It is the place where participants coordinate around a bounded mission without making one organization the necessary owner of every relationship.
