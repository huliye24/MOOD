# Treasury Design

Treasury is the resource coordination layer.

Potential pools:

- Ecosystem Fund
- Builder Grants
- Research Fund
- Infrastructure Fund
- Community Fund

Treasury exists to accelerate network growth.

---

> Pool sizes, sourcing, disbursement rules, custody, audit cadence,
> and on-chain anchoring are not specified in this document.
> Treasury activation requires a separate authoritative specification
> with verifiable evidence. No wallet addresses, pool balances, or
> distribution schedules are asserted here.
>
> Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §12, treasury is downstream of meaning. Per
> §Safety, treasury movement requires explicit human approval and
> must remain visibly verifiable.
