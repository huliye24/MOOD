# Rights System

MOOD separates:

Ownership

and

Participation Rights.

Participation rights emerge from:

- contribution
- reputation
- commitment

Rights may include:

- governance
- proposal creation
- ecosystem participation
- resource access

---

> This document describes the conceptual separation between ownership
> and participation rights. It does not enumerate, weight, or grant
> specific rights. Rights grants require authoritative specification
> and Canon alignment.
>
> Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §11, no application (including Moodify) holds
> canonical authority over the rights system. Rights are downstream
> of the protocol layer, which is downstream of the Canon.
