# Rights System

**Status:** Phase Zero foundational draft

**Authority:** Downstream of [`MOOD_CANON.md`](../../MOOD_CANON.md)

## Overview

Rights represent participation ability inside MOOD.

Rights may come from:

- Contribution
- Reputation
- Governance
- Capital participation
- Roles


## Examples

A contributor may receive:

- Proposal rights
- Verification rights
- Access rights
- Reward eligibility


## Principle

Rights should be explicit.

Participants should understand:

Why they have a right.

How they obtained it.

How it can change.
