# MOOD Blockchain Integration Architecture v0.1

## The Economic Settlement Layer of MOOD

Blockchain is not the identity of MOOD.

Blockchain is the settlement infrastructure that allows the network to coordinate value transparently.

MOOD separates:

- Culture
- Protocol
- Network
- Economy

Blockchain belongs to the economic layer.

---

# 1. Blockchain Role

```
MOOD Network

Culture
   |
Protocol
   |
Contribution
   |
Reputation
   |
Blockchain Settlement
   |
Economic Coordination
```

Blockchain provides:

- transparency
- ownership records
- token accounting
- incentives
- settlement

> Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §6.5, blockchain is the settlement layer and
> not the identity of MOOD. The chain cannot rewrite the canon,
> culture, or protocol.

---

# 2. BSC Integration

BSC is the initial blockchain environment for MOOD economic integration.

Potential functions:

- MOOD Token
- Treasury address
- Reward distribution
- Ecosystem allocation
- Community participation

Future multi-chain expansion may be considered.

> **No chain ID, no contract address, no RPC endpoint, no wallet
> address, and no mainnet/testnet designation is asserted in this
> Pack.** Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §Safety, any such information requires
> verifiable evidence and human approval gates before publication.
> BSC integration is therefore described as a planned direction, not
> an active deployment.

---

# 3. MOOD Token Role

MOOD Token should represent network participation and economic coordination.

Possible roles:

- ecosystem incentives
- contributor rewards
- treasury coordination
- governance participation
- network settlement

The token should serve the network.

The network should not exist only for the token.

> **No token is asserted as live, listed, distributed, or tradable.**
> No symbol, decimals, total supply, circulating supply, allocation
> schedule, or vesting curve is asserted. Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §12,
> the token is downstream of meaning and may not precede the
> protocol. Detailed token design in `MOOD_TOKEN_DESIGN.md` (planned).

# 5. Genesis Phase

Genesis phase focuses on:

- establishing trust
- documenting allocation
- creating transparency
- connecting early contributors

No future mechanism should be considered active unless implemented and verified.

> "Verified" means: (a) an authoritative specification with
> measurable evidence requirements exists, (b) a Canon check has
> been performed, (c) explicit human approval has been recorded for
> any irreversible on-chain action. None of these are claimed here.

---

# 6. Long Term Vision

Blockchain enables MOOD to become a global coordination network.

The goal is not speculation.

The goal is creating economic infrastructure for AI-native innovation.

> Vision is stated at the conceptual level. No timeline, roadmap
> commitment, or market expectation is asserted. The Canon does not
> authorize forecasts of price, adoption, or release dates.
