# MOOD Treasury Architecture

Treasury coordinates resources for ecosystem growth.

Potential categories:

```
Treasury

├── Builder Grants

├── Research Fund

├── Infrastructure Fund

├── Ecosystem Fund

└── Community Fund
```

Treasury principles:

- transparency
- long-term development
- ecosystem expansion

---

> **No treasury is asserted as active, funded, custodied, or
> operational.** No wallet address, pool size, custody arrangement,
> signer set, multisig threshold, audit cadence, or disbursement
> rule is asserted in this Pack.
>
> Per `MOOD_CANON.md` §12, treasury is downstream of meaning. Per
> Canon §Safety, treasury movement requires:
> 1. Authoritative specification with verifiable evidence.
> 2. Canon alignment review.
> 3. Explicit human approval for every movement.
>
> Until all three are recorded, the treasury categories above remain
> conceptual pool labels, not live pools.

## Conceptual Pool Labels

| Label | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Builder Grants | Reward contributors | conceptual |
| Research Fund | Support research output | conceptual |
| Infrastructure Fund | Support infrastructure | conceptual |
| Ecosystem Fund | Support ecosystem projects | conceptual |
| Community Fund | Support community programs | conceptual |

Pool activation, sourcing, and disbursement rules are planned and
remain unverified.
