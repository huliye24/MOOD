# Decision 001: Adopt MOODISM as the Civilizational Philosophy of MOOD

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Authority:** Repository maintainer  
**Proposal:** [MIP-CANON-001](../proposals/PROPOSAL_MOODISM_IDENTITY.md)

## Decision

MOODISM is adopted as the civilizational philosophy and value direction of
MOOD. MOOD remains the independent project, repository, and open coordination
world. MOOD Protocol is the institutional and technical expression of stable
canonical concepts. Tokens and other economic instruments remain downstream.

## Reason

The distinction allows the project to express a durable worldview without
allowing a website, application, protocol implementation, or token to become
the hidden definition of the whole project.

## Consequences

- `MOOD_CANON.md` advances from v0.1 to v0.2.
- MOODISM Manifesto 001 remains a Genesis Draft and cultural source; it does
  not independently activate mechanisms.
- Future code must identify the canonical concept and authority document it
  implements.
- Existing Token, Treasury, Governance, Airdrop, and Portal surfaces must
  continue distinguishing observable facts from inactive or proposed states.

## Reversibility

This is a conceptual amendment recorded in Git. It can be superseded only by a
later explicit Canon amendment that preserves the historical decision record.
