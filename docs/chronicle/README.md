# docs/chronicle

Chronicle holds living record of MOOD's ongoing Canon Amendment Proposals and
other long-form project proposals and explicit decisions. Draft proposals do
not modify [`MOOD_CANON.md`](../../MOOD_CANON.md); accepted decisions record
the human authority and amendment that did.

## Authority

Chronicle files do not modify the Canon merely by existing. A proposal may be
Draft, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, or Superseded. Accepted decisions
record why an explicit Canon amendment occurred; the amended Canon remains the
highest authority.

## Naming Convention

Proposal files use `PROPOSAL_<SCOPE>.md`. The scope indicates the subsystem
the proposal concerns (for example `FOUNDATION_NODE`,
`CONTRIBUTOR_LIFECYCLE`, `COMMUNITY_NODE`).

## Status Lifecycle

Proposals in this directory may transition through:

1. **Draft** — under authoring or active discussion
2. **Under Review** — submitted for Canon review
3. **Accepted** — accepted into `MOOD_CANON.md` and paired with a decision record
4. **Superseded** — replaced by a later proposal or amendment
5. **Withdrawn / Rejected** — no longer active

Until a proposal is **Accepted**, it carries no canonical authority.
