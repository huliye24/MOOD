# MOOD Governance Model

## Direction

MOOD governance is based on contribution, reputation, and participation.

---

## Governance Flow

```
Contribution

      ↓

Reputation

      ↓

Rights

      ↓

Participation

      ↓

Network Evolution
```

---

## Goals

- avoid centralized control
- reward builders
- protect long-term development
- encourage innovation

---

> This document is a directional model. Concrete rules, quorum,
> veto, escalation mechanisms, and decision authority remain planned.
> See `docs/architecture/09_governance-process.md` for the lifecycle
> description and `docs/architecture/02_protocol-architecture.md` §7
> for placement inside the protocol layer.
>

> mechanism descriptions remain at the principled level.
