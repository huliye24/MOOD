# Human AI Coexistence

AI introduces a new type of participant.

Future networks will include:

- Humans
- AI Agents
- Organizations
- Machines

The challenge is not only intelligence.

The challenge is:

Who is responsible?
Who contributed?
How can actions be verified?

MOOD treats AI agents as participants with:

- Identity
- Authority
- Execution records
- Reputation

Human judgment and machine capability cooperate through shared protocol rules.
