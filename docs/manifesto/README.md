# MOOD Manifesto

The cultural foundation of MOOD.

`MOOD_CANON.md` remains the repository's highest conceptual authority.
Manifestos express the cultural and civilizational direction of MOOD; a
manifesto marked as a draft does not amend the Canon or prove that any
protocol, governance, token, staking, or economic mechanism is active.
