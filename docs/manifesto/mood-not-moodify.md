# MOOD Manifesto

## MOOD Is Not Moodify

**Status:** Canonical Draft  
**Category:** Manifesto / Product Boundary  
**Protocol:** MOOD Protocol

MOOD is an open coordination protocol and digital world for human and machine agency.

Moodify is an independent sound-intelligence application that may use MOOD. It does not define MOOD, govern MOOD, or bound the domains in which MOOD may operate.

> MOOD is the protocol. Moodify is an independent application built on the protocol.

The relationship is directional:

```text
MOOD Protocol
  ↓
Open application boundary
  ↓
Moodify and other independent applications
```

MOOD enables Moodify. Moodify does not own MOOD.

Historical `Moodify`, `moodify-*`, and `MFY-*` identifiers may remain where changing them would break stored data, signed messages, schemas, contracts, deployment bindings, or reproducible evidence. Their presence is a compatibility fact, not a statement of product identity.
