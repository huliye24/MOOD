# MOODISM Manifesto 001 — Intellectual History

**Status:** Living version record  
**Authority:** Historical and cultural; subordinate to `MOOD_CANON.md`  
**Rule:** Published versions are immutable. New thought creates a new version; it does not overwrite an earlier one.

## Why the versions remain visible

MOOD treats Markdown and Git history as the public memory of a world becoming
more precise. A manifesto is not valuable only because of its latest wording.
Its revisions show which dangers became visible, which values moved closer to
the center, and which questions remained unresolved.

The archive therefore preserves roughness, repetition, tension, and correction
when they are evidence of genuine intellectual development. Editorial
cleanliness must not erase the path by which the project learned.

## Version lineage

```text
v0.1 — Contribution as the foundation of network value
  ↓
v0.2 — Human dignity and fundamental rights placed before contribution
  ↓
v0.3 — Dignity, freedom, and contribution become a civilizational triad
```

## v0.1 — Contribution becomes visible

**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Genesis Draft · preserved  
**Chinese:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_ZH.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_ZH.md)  
**English:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_EN.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_EN.md)

The first version establishes the central movement from platform order toward
contribution order:

```text
Contribution → Reputation → Rights → Shared Value
```

It defines contribution, responsibility, open protocols, human-machine
collaboration, anti-capture principles, and the distinction between MOODISM,
MOOD Protocol, and MOOD as an economic instrument.

Its unresolved tension is important: if contribution becomes the principal
source of institutional standing, what protects a person who cannot
contribute, refuses to contribute, or cannot be measured?

## v0.2 — Dignity limits the contribution order

**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Genesis Draft · current reading edition  
**Chinese:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.2_ZH.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.2_ZH.md)  
**English:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.2_EN.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.2_EN.md)

The second version does not abandon contribution. It places a moral floor
beneath it:

> Human dignity precedes contribution. Human rights do not need to be earned.

Its main developments are:

- Principle Zero: dignity and fundamental rights precede contribution;
- institutional rewards may differ while human standing remains equal;
- liberal, existentialist, romantic, and humanist foundations become explicit;
- algorithms may not define personhood or hold final authority over
  fundamental rights;
- protection of vulnerable people, due process, appeal, exit, rest, failure,
  and new beginnings become institutional requirements;
- efficiency is explicitly limited by human dignity.

The intellectual movement is therefore:

```text
v0.1: Contribution prevents status and capital from monopolizing value.
v0.2: Dignity prevents contribution from becoming a new system of domination.
```

## v0.3 — From a contribution order to a common world

**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Genesis Draft III · current reading edition  
**Chinese:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.3_ZH.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.3_ZH.md)  
**English:** [MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.3_EN.md](MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.3_EN.md)

The third version reorganizes the manifesto rather than merely extending it.
Its central structure becomes:

```text
Dignity → Freedom → Contribution
Existence → Encounter → Common World
```

Its principal developments are:

- dignity is made non-tradable, freedom non-replaceable, and contribution
  visible without becoming the price of personhood;
- value is divided into computable, deliberative, and invaluable forms;
- justice is divided into contributive, procedural, and restorative forms;
- the condition of vulnerable people becomes a measure of civilization;
- human-machine relations become a civilizational covenant rather than only a
  contribution-accountability problem;
- governance receives constitutional, protocol, and plural boundaries;
- common wealth and the public world move closer to the center;
- the text takes on a more symbolic and civilizational architecture through
  the recurring movement from one, to two, to three, to the many.

The intellectual arc now reads:

```text
v0.1: Contribution challenges inherited status and platform ownership.
v0.2: Dignity and human rights limit the power of contribution systems.
v0.3: Dignity, freedom, and contribution generate a plural common world.
```

The v0.3 English metadata describes the document as a “Civilizational Canon.”
Within this repository it remains a cultural Genesis Draft subordinate to
`MOOD_CANON.md`; the phrase records the author's conception of the text and
does not silently change repository authority.

## Rule for v0.4 and later

1. Never edit v0.1 or v0.2 to make a later version look inevitable.
2. Add new files named `MOODISM_MANIFESTO_001_V0.N_ZH.md` and `_EN.md`.
3. Record what changed, why it changed, and what contradiction produced it.
4. Preserve disagreement and unfinished questions.
5. Update the website's current reading edition without removing older routes.
6. Record hashes after the files enter the repository.
7. A manifesto version does not amend the Canon automatically.
