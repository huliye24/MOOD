# MOOD Governance Model

## Direction

MOOD governance is based on contribution, reputation, and participation.

---

## Governance Flow

```
Contribution

      ↓

Reputation

      ↓

Rights

      ↓

Participation

      ↓

Network Evolution
```

---

## Goals

- avoid centralized control
- reward builders
- protect long-term development
- encourage innovation

---
