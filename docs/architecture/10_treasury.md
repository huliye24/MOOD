# Treasury Design

Treasury is the resource coordination layer.

Potential pools:

- Ecosystem Fund
- Builder Grants
- Research Fund
- Infrastructure Fund
- Community Fund

Treasury exists to accelerate network growth.

---

> Pool sizes, sourcing, disbursement rules, custody, and audit cadence
> are not specified in this document. Treasury activation requires a
> separate authoritative specification with verifiable evidence.
> No wallet addresses or chain data are asserted here.
