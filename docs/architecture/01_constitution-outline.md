# MOOD Constitution Outline

## Purpose

Define the principles of the MOOD network.

---

# Principles

## Contribution Over Position

Value comes from creation.

## Network Over Hierarchy

Innovation emerges from collaboration.

## Open Participation

Anyone can contribute.

## Reputation Over Resume

Verified contribution creates trust.

## AI Native Organization

Humans and AI agents collaborate.

---

# Governance Direction

MOOD governance should be based on:

- Contribution
- Reputation
- Participation
- Transparency

Not only ownership or status.
